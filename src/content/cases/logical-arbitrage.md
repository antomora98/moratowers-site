## Hypothesis

Related markets on one prediction-market venue (Polymarket) must price consistently. Where they do not, the gap is free money.

## Test

860 events scanned for related markets whose prices disagreed. Eight raw opportunities. Three were never arbitrage: nested thresholds, cumulative deadlines, and outcome sets that did not cover every case. Five were real. None could fill a 500-dollar order.

## Cause of death

Capacity. The gaps exist and they are real, and they are too small to trade at the size tested. The verdict rests on an exact count over all 860 events scanned, not on an interval, which the audit's plan allows for a count. The audit's check of this cause: consistent with its own record.

## Lesson

Count what would have filled before you count a gap as profit.
