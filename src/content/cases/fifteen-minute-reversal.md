## Hypothesis

On Polymarket's fifteen-minute bitcoin markets, the next window reverses the last one more often than not. Bet the reversal.

## Test

Reversal rate 50.7%, 95% interval 48.4% to 53.0%, against a coin flip at 50%. The interval contains the coin flip. Polymarket's international venue, where these markets trade, is not open to US traders.

## Cause of death

Blocked. Nothing in the data separates this from chance, and the venue could not be traded from here in any case. This is a record, not an invitation to trade it. The audit's check of this cause: consistent with its own record.

## Lesson

A coin flip with a story is still a coin flip.
