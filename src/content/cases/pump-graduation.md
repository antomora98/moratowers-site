## Hypothesis

On pump.fun a memecoin graduates when it crosses a visible threshold, and the attention a graduation draws arrives after it. So, by hand: buy five minutes after a coin graduates, sell at sixty minutes, and let the late crowd pay.

## Test

A replay of the rule on a public record of graduations in a fixed window: 4,158 complete round trips after dropping the rows the corpus itself flags as synthetic and the 94 with no liquidity reading, each scored net of fees and price impact, with a bootstrap interval on the mean. Then the same rule on the larger pools only, an exploratory cut, where costs take a smaller share of each trade.
